Event text

Paste the event text you copied from a website, email or message - or share a page to CalSnap from your phone's Share menu.

Review details

Leave the end time empty and the event is created one hour long.

The reminder is written into the downloaded .ics file. Google Calendar does not accept a reminder from a link - set it there after the event is created. Whether a notification actually appears depends on your calendar application and its settings.

Opening Google Calendar only opens a prefilled page. Review it there and press Save - until you do, nothing has been added to any calendar.

CalSnap runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you paste is uploaded, and no account is needed. Extracted details are a best guess from your text: always check them before saving.
